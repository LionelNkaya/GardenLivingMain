Helene's Mission

Helene Moore is French and moved to California in 2014. When Helene was living in Paris, she worked for 13 years as an Assistant Duty Manager for a Currency exchange office American Express during 8 years and then Travelex for 5 years. Helene started as a simple cashier. After only one year, she became office manager for a while and then became Assistant Duty Manager. During this year, Helene learned how to manage a team of 55 people and different Currency Offices at Roissy Charles de Gaulle Airport. She learned how to solve different complicated situation and maintained the daily target. Even though she liked her job, Helene always wanted to work in the medical field.

For almost 2 years, before she moved to Los Angeles, Helene worked in an Assisted Living in Paris. After she moved to Los Angeles, Helene acquired almost 15 years experience as a caregiver with a varied background in providing individual care to people of all ages and conditions.

Helene has worked with different cases sometimes complicated cases like Dementia, Parkinson and Alzheimer disease... or simple cases requiring just companionship. Helene's expertise includes caring for elderly clients with medical conditions such as COPD, cancer, stroke, and diabetes, as well as bedridden and hospice patients.

Because her passion for helping people was too important, she decided only in 2020 with her husband to have their own Residential Care Facility for Elderly.
